# Code of conduct

Sodium Remote is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to work on. This code applies to every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where you speak for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good faith, and disagree about the work, not the person.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience. Explain, and don't talk down.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention that isn't welcome.
- Publishing other people's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for applying this code. They may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks it, with a short explanation where that is safe.

To report a problem, write to **enterprise@sodiumos.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential from the person reported.
